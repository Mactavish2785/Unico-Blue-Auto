Unico Blue Automotive Group is a used vehicle dealership serving Imperial, Jefferson County, and nearby communities throughout the St. Louis area.

WHO WE ARE

A Dealership Built Around Affordable Transportation

Unico Blue Automotive Group was created with a straightforward purpose: provide reliable used vehicles at budget-friendly prices.

We understand that many customers are not looking for an expensive luxury vehicle or a large monthly payment. They need dependable transportation to get to work, take care of their families, run errands, or simply get where they need to go.

Our inventory is focused on affordable cars, trucks, SUVs, and vans that can serve those everyday transportation needs.

OUR MISSION

To provide reliable, affordable used vehicles while making the buying process straightforward and accessible.

We aim to serve working individuals and families, first-time vehicle buyers, customers rebuilding their credit, and people who simply need an inexpensive vehicle they can depend on.

BEFORE THE SALE

Preparing Vehicles for Their Next Owner

Before a vehicle is offered for sale, it may undergo mechanical evaluation, applicable safety and emissions inspections, necessary maintenance, and detailing.

Our objective is to understand the condition of the vehicles we offer and prepare them for the next customer.

Mechanical Evaluation Vehicle systems and condition reviewed.
Safety Inspection Applicable Missouri requirements addressed.
Emissions Inspection Completed when applicable to the vehicle.
Maintenance Necessary maintenance addressed as appropriate.
Detailing Vehicles cleaned and prepared for sale.
